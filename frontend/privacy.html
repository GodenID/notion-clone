Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

NookSpace is a self-hosted workspace. You own your data. This policy describes what NookSpace collects, how it is used, and your rights.

1. What we collect

  • Account data: email, name, password hash (scrypt), and an optional avatar color. Passwords are never stored in plain text.
  • Content: pages, blocks, databases, comments, teamspaces, and uploaded files you create.
  • Session data: an opaque session token in an HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax cookie, with a 30-day expiry.
  • Usage metadata: IP address and user agent, recorded in the audit log for security (login attempts, mutations).

2. How we use it

Your data is used solely to operate your workspace: authentication, rendering pages, search indexing, realtime sync, and file storage. We do not sell, share, or monetize your data. There is no advertising.

3. Where it lives

In self-hosted mode, everything lives on the machine or server you control. In Cloudflare mode, data lives in your own Cloudflare account (D1 database, R2 bucket). NookSpace has no central server and no access to your data.

4. Cookies

NookSpace uses one cookie — nookspace_session — for authentication. It is HttpOnly (not readable by JavaScript), SameSite=Lax, and expires after 30 days. No tracking or analytics cookies are set.

5. Third-party services

If you enable OAuth (Google or GitHub), those providers handle authentication and may collect data per their own privacy policies. NookSpace only stores the email, name, and provider user ID returned by them. If you use the S3 storage driver, your files are stored in your own S3-compatible bucket.

6. Your rights

  • Export: your data is in standard SQLite + JSON. You can copy the database file or export pages as Markdown at any time.
  • Delete: deleting your account removes all your pages, comments, uploads, and sessions. Teamspaces you own are transferred or deleted.
  • Access: you can view your audit log at any time via the command palette ("View audit log").

7. Security

Passwords are hashed with scrypt (Node) or PBKDF2 (Cloudflare). Sessions are server-side and revocable. Rate limiting protects against brute-force. Two-factor authentication (TOTP) is available. We recommend running NookSpace behind HTTPS in production.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be noted in the repository's changelog. Continued use constitutes acceptance.

9. Contact

Questions? Reach out via Telegram or open an issue on GitHub.
